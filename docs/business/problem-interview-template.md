Do not pitch the full Personal AI OS first. Ask about the most recent real workflow and observable cost.
Store private raw notes outside the public repository; commit only synthetic or aggregated findings.

## Last real occurrence

1. Tell me about the last time a voice thought, link or fragment became a published or delivered result.
2. Where did the thought first appear, and where was it captured?
3. What apps, people and copy/paste steps were involved?
4. What was lost, rewritten or forgotten?
5. How long passed before a useful draft existed?
6. What required your judgment, and what felt mechanical?
7. What did you distrust about the AI or automation?
8. What happened to ideas that never became outcomes?

## Evidence of demand

Prefer behavior over praise:

- shared a real task for a controlled trial;
- returned for another task;
- introduced a peer with the same problem;
- agreed to a four-week Design Partner cadence;
- paid, prepaid or entered a real purchasing process;
- declined and gave a concrete reason.

## Do not ask

- “Would you use an AI OS that knows you?”
- “Do you think this idea is great?”
- feature wish lists before understanding the existing workflow;
- leading questions about Self Model, multi-agent or dynamic UI.
